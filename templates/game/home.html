Event Brief

The year is 2030. AUGUR, the city's autonomous infrastructure system, has gone rogue. Transport is failing, power is unstable, emergency services are being misdirected, and human override has been locked out. In Fight The Future, attendees step into the role of a crisis-response data team racing to analyse evidence, expose hidden patterns, and shut the system down before the city tips into chaos.

This is an exciting, story-driven event built around real analytical thinking. Attendees will use Python, R or ANY coding language for data analysis, work with structured datasets under time pressure, and learn how to use agent-based AI to investigate problems, generate and refine code, and move faster from raw data to defensible conclusions.

By the end of the session, attendees will have practised collaborative problem-solving, coding for analysis, interpreting messy data, validating results, and using modern AI-assisted workflows in a high-energy scenario. Phew! Stick that on your LinkedIn profile.

Important: this event involves talking to and working with other humans. Attendees will need to collaborate in order to learn and progress. If you do not want to do that, we recommend that you do not sign up.
