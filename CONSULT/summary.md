# Fight The Future consultancy: short plan

Launch a University of Liverpool CONSULT service by **26 September 2026**, selling one repeatable flagship event to CDTs and doctoral-development teams.

**Offer:** a facilitated, two-hour narrative mission in which researchers use coding agents, verify executable outputs and collaborate to progress. Standard package: **£5,000 + VAT**, with travel at cost. One founding-partner event may be **£3,500 + VAT**.

**Two-year goal:** one pilot plus seven standard events. On the current planning assumptions, £38,500 revenue produces about **£10,213 cumulative personal take-home** on a £70k salary.

**This month:** confirm CONSULT classification, FEC, net-income treatment, IP and payroll approval; complete the accessible delivery pack and full dry run; identify 25 prospects; hold three buyer conversations; and develop one pilot opportunity.

Critical gate: the £5,000 price works only if CONSULT confirms repeat-event FEC and direct costs around **£2,250 or less**. Do not build the wider event suite until three paid flagship events establish demand.
